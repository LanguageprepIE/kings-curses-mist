# Of Kings, Curses and Mist / De reyes, maldiciones y niebla

**An illustrated bilingual collection of Irish myths and legends.**

Created and curated by **David Martín Guerrero**. First published **7 November 2026**.

This educational literary project retells legends from Ireland's traditional oral and literary heritage in newly written English and Spanish adaptations. David Martín Guerrero directs and reviews the editorial work. AI tools have assisted with story drafting, translation and the generation of original illustrations. The audio currently uses browser text-to-speech.

Historical and literary research references for *The Children of Lir* are listed in `editorial/01-reader-storyboard.md` and in the site's post-story notes. Referencing a traditional story does not imply that modern editions, translations or illustrations by other creators are being reproduced or licensed.

The repository is public to host the reading experience; no open-source or reuse licence has yet been granted for the original materials. Please contact the project creator before reusing them.
