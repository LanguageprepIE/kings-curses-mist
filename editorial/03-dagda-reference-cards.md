# Chapter 03 — Mythological reference cards and art continuity
**Collection:** [LIBRO] Of Kings, Curses and Mist
**Status:** Bilingual reference material for reader; illustrative character designs are artistic interpretations, not historical reconstructions.
**Primary source:** UCC CELT, *Cath Maige Tuired*, §§163–164: https://celt.ucc.ie/published/T300010.html

## People / Pueblos
### Tuatha Dé Danann
EN: A supernatural people of early Irish legend associated with skill, enchantment and struggles over Ireland. The Dagda, Lugh and Ogma belong to their stories.
ES: Pueblo sobrenatural de las antiguas leyendas irlandesas, asociado a la magia, las artes y las disputas por el dominio de Irlanda. Entre sus figuras aparecen el Dagda, Lugh y Ogma.
Appears / Aparece: I (Children of Lir); III (Dagda’s Harp).
### Fomorians / Fomorianos
EN: Powerful rivals of the Tuatha Dé Danann in stories of ancient conflict. Their appearances and family relationships differ among accounts.
ES: Poderosos rivales de los Tuatha Dé Danann en relatos de antiguos conflictos. Su aspecto y sus relaciones familiares varían según la tradición.
Appears / Aparece: III.

## Characters / Personajes
### The Dagda / El Dagda
EN: A prominent figure of the Tuatha Dé Danann, associated with enormous strength, abundance and magic. In this story, he calls his harp by its old names.
ES: Figura destacada de los Tuatha Dé Danann, vinculada a la fuerza, la abundancia y la magia. En esta leyenda llama al arpa por sus antiguos nombres.
Visual continuity (provisional): very tall and broad; curly long brown/greying hair and thick beard; dark forest-green cloak, earth-tone tunic and rounded bronze brooch; powerful hands, expressive but not caricatured.
### Lugh
EN: Skilled champion of the Tuatha Dé Danann, one of the three companions who retrieve the harp.
ES: Hábil campeón de los Tuatha Dé Danann; acompaña al Dagda para recuperar el arpa.
Visual continuity (provisional): younger adult; curly reddish-brown hair; slim athletic build; slate-blue cloak, small round brooch; alert eyes.
### Ogma
EN: A mighty warrior among the Tuatha Dé Danann, who accompanies the Dagda and Lugh.
ES: Poderoso guerrero de los Tuatha Dé Danann que acompaña al Dagda y a Lugh.
Visual continuity (provisional): mature, broad-shouldered, rugged bearded figure; long brown-grey hair, fur or rough-wool mantle, sword.
### Uaithne / Úaithne
EN: The Dagda’s harper in the *Cath Maige Tuired* episode, captured by the Fomorians. Not identified here as the harp’s own name.
ES: Arpista del Dagda en *Cath Maige Tuired*, capturado por los fomorianos. Aquí no se presenta como el nombre del instrumento.
Illustration: not present in the three selected scenes, so do not invent a portrait yet.
### Bres and Elatha / Bres y Elatha
EN: Figures in the Fomorian feasting hall; Bres is described as Elatha’s son in the cited episode.
ES: Personajes presentes en el banquete fomoriano; Bres aparece como hijo de Elatha en el episodio citado.
Visual continuity (provisional): Bres a dark-haired, high-status adult with bronze circlet and maroon cloak; Elatha not visually defined.

## Objects / Objetos
### The Dagda’s Harp / El arpa del Dagda
EN: A magical harp that returns when called and plays three transformative strains: sorrow, joy and sleep. Its two names in the source are Daur Dá Bláo and Cóir Cetharchair.
ES: Arpa mágica que regresa cuando se la llama y ejecuta tres melodías: tristeza, alegría y sueño. Sus dos nombres en la fuente son Daur Dá Bláo y Cóir Cetharchair.
Visual continuity: large carved golden-brown wooden frame, graceful curved pillar, fine strings and restrained ornamentation. Avoid representing it as a modern concert harp.

## Places / Lugares
### Mag Tuired
EN: The mythological battle associated with the conflict between the Tuatha Dé Danann and the Fomorians.
ES: Batalla legendaria vinculada al enfrentamiento entre los Tuatha Dé Danann y los fomorianos.
Do not equate a fictionalized hall interior with documented archaeology.
