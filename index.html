An illustrated collection of Irish myths & legends · Created and curated by David Martín Guerrero · First published 7 November 2026

About the work and AI assistance

Original literary retellings of traditional Irish legends, developed and reviewed under the editorial direction of David Martín Guerrero. AI tools have assisted with writing, translation, and the creation of original illustrations. The underlying legends belong to Irish oral and literary tradition.
